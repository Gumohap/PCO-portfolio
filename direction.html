My direction in IT

Exploring my direction

Two areas I have explored are Data Science and Software Engineering. Learning about them helps me understand which kind of work fits my interests and how I could move towards cybersecurity.

Data Science

Data Science is about using data to answer questions and find useful patterns. Data scientists collect and prepare data, analyze it with tools such as programming and statistics, and explain what they discover with visualizations or reports. This work can help organizations make better decisions. I find it interesting that data can reveal patterns, but I am more drawn to understanding how systems are built and protected.

Software Engineering

Software Engineering is about designing, building, testing, and maintaining software. Engineers work out what users need, write and review code, test that it works, and improve it over time. I can see a connection to my interest in logical puzzles: a large problem can be broken into smaller steps and solved through testing ideas. I am especially interested in secure software and how developers can reduce security weaknesses.

My current preference

Cybersecurity

Cybersecurity is my preferred direction because I want to use logical problem-solving to help protect systems and information. It connects with Software Engineering through secure design and coding, but it is broader than software alone: cybersecurity can also involve networks, devices, and responding to security incidents. I would like to explore roles such as security analyst and learn which skills are needed to do this work well.

A possible future

Future me — four years from now

In four years, I hope to have completed my ICT studies and be starting a career in cybersecurity. I would like to work as a junior cybersecurity specialist or security analyst in an organization with a dedicated IT security team. I want to help find security risks, understand how attacks happen, and protect systems and information. By then, I hope to have built practical skills through my studies, projects, and an internship, while learning from people with more experience. I also want to be better at managing my time and breaking difficult problems into steps. My plans may develop as I learn more, but cybersecurity is the direction I want to explore.
