My goals

My student SWOT analysis

This analysis helps me understand what I can build on, what I can improve, and what opportunities I can use during my studies.

Strengths

  • Logical problem-solving: I have enjoyed solving logical puzzles since I was a kid, and programming gives me a similar challenge.
  • Persistence: When code does not work straight away, I can keep trying ideas and look for what went wrong.

Opportunities

  • Internship: An ICT internship can give me experience using what I learn on real tasks in a workplace.
  • Minor: I can choose a minor to explore a subject in more depth and build knowledge for a future ICT career.

Weaknesses

  • Procrastination: I sometimes leave schoolwork until the last minute instead of starting early.
  • Breaking work into steps: I need to divide bigger assignments into smaller tasks so I know what to do first.

Threats

  • Overlapping deadlines: Several assignments due around the same time can make it harder to give each one enough attention.
  • Low motivation: When a task takes longer than expected or progress feels slow, it may be harder for me to stay motivated.

My SMART goals

Use my problem-solving strength

By 31 December 2026, I will complete one programming or cybersecurity exercise each week (e.g. Python, TryHackMe). I will keep a list and tick off each exercise so I can see my progress. This uses my problem-solving strength to build skills for future IT/security courses

Start assignments earlier

Every Sunday by 18:00 until 31 December 2026, I will list my upcoming assignments, break each one into at least three smaller tasks, and add those tasks to my weekly schedule. This tackles my procrastination so overlapping deadlines don't pile up

My mission this year

Building good study habits

This year, I want to grow as an ICT student by using my logical problem-solving skills and persistence. I will keep practising by completing one programming or logic exercise each week. I am also working on starting assignments earlier by planning them every Sunday and dividing them into smaller tasks. By 31 December 2026, I want these habits to help me make steady progress towards a future in cybersecurity.
