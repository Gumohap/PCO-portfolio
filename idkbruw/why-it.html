Why IT?

Why IT and ICT studies?

WHY?

I didn’t choose IT with a big plan. In high school, I wasn’t interested in other subjects, so I chose IT and graduated in it. As I studied, I began to think it might be the right path. Since I was a kid, I’ve enjoyed logical puzzles. Programming gives me a similar feeling: looking for clues, trying ideas, and finding a solution. That keeps me curious.

HOW?

I want to keep learning by practising, building projects, and understanding how systems work. I chose HZ University of Applied Sciences because I learn best by doing practical work rather than just studying theory. The project-based approach at HZ gives me the chance to apply what I learn directly on real assignments.

Cybersecurity interests me because it combines logic with real problems: finding weaknesses and helping protect people and their information. I know I have a lot to learn, but I’m ready to work at it.

WHAT?

I’m studying ICT at HZ to build a strong base in software development and technology. My long-term goal is to finish my degree, gain practical experience through an IT internship, and work towards becoming a cybersecurity specialist.

Beyond the tech side, I’m also attracted to the career flexibility in IT, the possibility of working remotely or traveling, and the good job stability and growth opportunities in the field.
